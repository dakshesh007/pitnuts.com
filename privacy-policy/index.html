Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PITNUTS (“Pitnuts”, “we”, “us” or “our”), having its place of business at B-556, Sitaram, Archana School Road, Surat - 395010, Gujarat, India, collects, uses, stores and protects information when you visit or interact with our website https://pitnuts.com (the “Website”).

This policy is published in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”) and the rules made thereunder. By using the Website you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the Website.

1. Who We Are

Pitnuts is an Indian health and wellness brand that markets nutraceutical products. Our products are sold through third-party online marketplaces such as Amazon. The Website is an informational website; it does not sell products directly, does not require you to create an account, and does not process payments.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Voluntarily

The Website does not have a server-side contact form. When you choose to contact us, you do so through third-party channels such as WhatsApp, email or social media. In that case we receive the information you send us, which may include your name, phone number, email address and the content of your message. We use this information only to respond to your enquiry, feedback or complaint.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider and any analytics tools we may use can automatically collect limited technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, referring URL, pages viewed and the date and time of your visit. This information is used in aggregate to keep the Website secure, understand how it is used and improve it. It is not used to identify you personally.

2.3 Cookies

The Website may use essential cookies and similar technologies that are necessary for it to function, and, if enabled, analytics cookies to measure traffic. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some features of the Website.

2.4 Sensitive Personal Data

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data or information such as passwords, financial information, health records, or biometric data through the Website. Please do not share such information with us over WhatsApp or email.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your questions, feedback and consumer complaints.
  • To provide information about our products and their availability.
  • To operate, secure, maintain and improve the Website.
  • To comply with applicable laws, regulations and lawful requests from authorities.
  • With your consent, to send you updates about product launches (you may opt out at any time).

4. Legal Basis and Consent

Under the DPDP Act, we process your personal data on the basis of your consent, given when you voluntarily contact us, or for certain legitimate uses permitted by law, such as responding to a request you have initiated or complying with a legal obligation. You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us using the details in Section 11. Withdrawal will not affect processing carried out before withdrawal.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. We may share information only:

  • With service providers who help us run the Website and communicate with you (for example, hosting, email and messaging providers), who are bound to use it only for those purposes.
  • With government or law-enforcement authorities where required by applicable law or court order.
  • To protect the rights, property or safety of Pitnuts, our customers or the public.

6. Third-Party Websites and Services

The Website contains links to third-party platforms including Amazon, WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook. When you purchase our products on Amazon or contact us through WhatsApp, your data is processed by those platforms under their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage you to review those policies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites or services.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including to resolve your enquiry or complaint, and to meet legal, accounting or reporting requirements. When it is no longer required, it is deleted or anonymised.

8. Data Security

We follow reasonable security practices and procedures, as required under Indian law, to protect the information we hold from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights

As a Data Principal under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is processed.
  • Request correction, completion or updating of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Request erasure of your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Withdraw consent previously given.
  • Nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity.
  • Raise a grievance with us and, if unresolved, approach the Data Protection Board of India.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Grievance Officer using the details in Section 11.

10. Children’s Privacy

The Website is not directed to children under 18 years of age and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly. Our products are not recommended for children; please see product labels.
